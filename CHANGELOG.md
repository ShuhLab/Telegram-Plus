# Telegram+ — Release notes

## 2026-11-25 — Free personal start (verified production)

**User problem:** the official Telegram+ bot previously greeted new visitors with a closed-pilot notice, forcing them to request manual access even for occasional personal use.

**What changed:** the production bot [@M2G_AIManager_bot](https://telegram-plus.m2g.pro/go/start) now offers self-service personal onboarding after acceptance of Terms and separate personal-data consent. The public site and bot profile explain the actual allowance: **20 AI units for up to 90 days**, with a limit of **100 replies total and 20 per day**, and consumption based on actual AI work. Daily new-user capacity remains limited. The old test bot is isolated from the production signup flow.

**Safety and availability:** no automatic charges, no mandatory subscription. Paid top-ups remain disabled. Access to other users' Telegram Business chats is not granted by personal signup and still requires a separately reviewed connection. The Telegram+ website currently has RU and EN interfaces; the ES video does not imply a fully translated Spanish website.
