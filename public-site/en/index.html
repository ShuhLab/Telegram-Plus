BEFORE WE START

Straight answers.

Is this a separate messenger?

No. Telegram+ is an assistant for Telegram conversations. Setup and feature availability are discussed during the pilot. Working with Business chats requires the corresponding Telegram configuration.

Will it reply in every chat?

Not automatically. You choose its scope: selected conversations, new enquiries and exceptions. Access is configured by the owner.

Can a real manager take over?

Yes. Handing complex questions to a person is part of the Telegram+ workflow. During the pilot we configure who receives an enquiry and when intervention is needed.

Can I buy access now?

Public sales are not open yet. You can discuss joining the pilot. Payments remain disabled until onboarding, charges and refunds have been verified.

How do I report a problem or share an idea?

Contact the M2G team through the link below. A dedicated Telegram+ feedback channel is planned; its setup is discussed during the pilot. Never send passwords or private customer conversations.

Do I need to write code or program every reply?

No. The verified owner can describe the task, boundaries, and tone in normal language, assign the right context, and choose authorized chats. No reply-by-reply programming is needed.

Can I add a price list or an entire folder?

The pilot supports PDF, DOCX, XLSX, CSV, TXT, and JSON. Private projects support local folder and ZIP batch uploads. The owner must approve any materials made available to Business conversations.

Can I connect Google Sheets or Google Drive?

Google Sheets requires a separate owner-authorized connection. Documents from Google Drive can be exported and uploaded. We do not offer automatic access to every file on a Google Drive account.

What happens when prices change?

Update the relevant file or connected spreadsheet, review the new information, and approve its use. Live availability or bookings require a separate check against the current source.

Can it check live stock or open calendar times?

Google Sheets can read current catalog values after separate authorization. Google Calendar can check the busy/free status of a selected time interval; event creation requires approval. Google OAuth is not enabled for this public pilot yet.
