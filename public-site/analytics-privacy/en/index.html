Analytics.
Your choice.

The public Telegram+ website uses its own aggregated visit statistics and two third-party analytics services, which load only after visitor consent. The owner dashboard, payment pages and customer conversation contents are not sent to these trackers.

What works without consent

Our server aggregates page views, referral source categories and clicks on the main site links. This first-party counter does not use cookies, advertising-profile IDs or conversation data. The web server can technically receive IP addresses and browser information while serving requests, but the aggregate counter does not store them.

Which services load after consent

  • Google Analytics 4 — stream G-LRFH7DRXW2: page views and standard device/traffic-source data. Browser identifiers may be stored in cookies after consent. Ad personalization and Google Signals are disabled.
  • Yandex Metrica — counter 114510196: page views and technical site measurements. Session replay (Webvisor), click maps and automatic outbound-link tracking are disabled.

For international visitors, the script may use Yandex's documented alternative CDN domain when accessible. The website works even when third-party tracking is blocked.

Information sent to analytics providers

After consent, browsers may transmit the URL of a public page, visit time, device/browser information, approximate region and connection IP, together with provider-specific analytics identifiers. These providers process data under their own policies, potentially across borders. We do not intentionally send private Telegram Business messages, company documents or payment details to analytics.

How to decline or withdraw

On your first visit select “Essential only” to leave external scripts off. Your choice is stored in your browser. Use the “Analytics settings” button at the bottom of the public website to change your choice. If you withdraw consent, the page reloads without the external scripts. You can also clear site data or block analytics scripts in your browser.
